Deal type

New clients pay the one-time setup fee. Extensions renew at the platform fee only.

Employees managed

Employee commitments keep the quote aligned with the client’s expected workforce usage.

Annual hires

Annual hiring commitments keep the quote aligned with the client’s expected recruitment usage. Hires beyond the tier’s bundled cap add a small overage.

Branches / locations needed

Included up to the tier’s cap. Extra branches beyond that add a small annual overage.

Products

Both included in the platform fee. ATS only is 80% of list price; HRIS only is 65% — each tier's own minimum-margin floor still applies.

Add-on modules

Payroll and Attendance are included free once HRIS is fully active. Full HRIS upgrade is Essential-only — Growth and Enterprise already include full HRIS.

Discount on platform fee — 0% — list price

Discounts apply only to the platform fee and are capped at 15%. Platform fee never drops below that tier's own minimum-margin floor, combined with any other reduction. Setup and add-on pricing are never discounted.
